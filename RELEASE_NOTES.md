VRChat Speech Assistant Deluxe 1.31.0 release candidate 1

A new conversation-first workspace replaces the uneven scrolling card columns.
Listening, STOP speech, current voice/language pair and four language presets stay
at the top. Activity, warnings and errors remain fixed at the bottom.

Six tabs separate Conversation, Soundboard, History, Voice & languages, Roleplay
and Settings. Conversation keeps the shared transcript/manual editor, send/edit
controls and large reactions together. Chatbox preview is expandable with a page
count visible while folded. Separate display text expands only when selected.
History now updates directly in its tab, including replay, load and audio export;
Soundboard has a playback library alongside the existing detailed settings.

A saved Compact mode hides tabs and advanced options for VR, retaining the editor,
send controls, reactions and transport/status bars. Drafts and output settings
survive mode changes. Settings use aligned forms and independent scrolling.
Small windows or many favorites can still require conversation scrolling.

Also included: right-click shortcut assignment/removal for primary controls,
language buttons and favorite reactions; duplicate checks and a small offline
Windows/VRChat default-key warning catalogue; editable PL→PL, PL→EN, EN→EN and
PL→JA presets with individual shortcuts. Cross-language presets require DeepL;
remembered output voices/models and drafts are preserved.

Windows and Linux builds share these changes. Replace your application files;
existing voice, provider, audio and roleplay settings remain in your user folder.
Validation covers tab navigation, fixed controls, compact/preview persistence,
live history, Soundboard playback, shortcut capture/conflicts and language routing.
Both frozen builds also exercise the new workspace and Compact mode.
